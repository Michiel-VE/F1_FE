Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Controller & Contact

This hobbyist project is operated independently via michielve.be. For privacy or data deletion requests, contact the operator through the contact details listed on michielve.be.

2. Data We Collect & Legal Basis

When you sign in with Google, we receive and store basic account info (such as your name, email address, and avatar) along with your season predictions and group pool activity. We process this data to provide the requested prediction/pool features (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and keep the service secure and free of abuse (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

3. Service Providers & Cookies

Google provides OAuth authentication, and the application and database are hosted on AWS in Stockholm (EU/EEA), with any cross-border transfers outside the EEA subject to applicable transfer safeguards. We use essential session/auth cookies and browser storage needed for login and UI state; we do not sell personal data.

4. Retention & Your Rights

Data is kept while your account or season predictions are active, or until you request deletion. Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export your personal data, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (such as the authority in your country of residence or work).
